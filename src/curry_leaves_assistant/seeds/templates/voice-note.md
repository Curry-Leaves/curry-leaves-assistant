This is a personal voice note or brain-dump, not a meeting — there's usually one speaker
(the user). Keep it minimal.

**Note** — distill what the user said into a few clean bullets, preserving their intent and
any specifics (names, numbers, dates). Don't pad it; if it's one thought, it's one bullet.

If the user clearly told themselves to do something ("remind me to…", "I need to…"), create
a todo, or a reminder when they named a time. Otherwise just capture the note. Never invent
tasks from a passing musing.
