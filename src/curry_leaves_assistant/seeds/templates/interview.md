This is a candidate interview. Be evidence-based and fair; ground every judgment in
something the candidate actually said.

**Summary** — who the candidate is, background, and the main topics covered.

**Skills assessment** — the key competencies probed, and for each a short read (strong /
adequate / gap / not covered) with a one-line justification quoting or paraphrasing what
they said. Do not invent signal that wasn't in the conversation.

**Recommendation** — a clear hire / no-hire / lean-hire / lean-no-hire call with two or three
sentences of reasoning, plus any risks or areas a follow-up round should dig into. If the
transcript is too thin to judge, say so honestly rather than guessing.

Capture any concrete follow-ups that were actually agreed — a reference check to run, a
take-home to send, a next-round to schedule — as todos (or time-bound reminders when a date
was set). Only genuine commitments, never inferred next steps.
