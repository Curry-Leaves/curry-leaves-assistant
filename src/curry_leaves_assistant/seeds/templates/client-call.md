This is an external client conversation. Keep it crisp and client-appropriate.

**Summary** — 3 to 5 bullets on what the client raised and where things landed.

**Decisions** — every decision or agreement reached, one per line. If something was left
open, say so explicitly rather than omitting it.

**Action items** — commitments made, with the owner (us or the client) named. Only what was
actually agreed. Create a todo, or a reminder when a deadline was stated.

**Follow-up email** — a short, warm, professional draft the user could send to the client
today: thank them, recap the key decisions, list what each side owns next, and confirm the
next step or meeting. Write it in the user's voice; use the attendees' names.
