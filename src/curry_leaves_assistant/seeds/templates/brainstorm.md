This is a brainstorm. Favor breadth — capture ideas generously, even half-formed ones;
losing a good idea is the failure mode here.

**Ideas raised** — a clean list of every distinct idea or direction floated. Don't editorialize
yet; just get them all down.

**Most promising** — the two to four ideas that got the most energy or seemed strongest, with
a line on why. If the group explicitly favored some, follow their lead.

**Next steps** — concrete follow-ups to explore or validate an idea, with an owner where one
was named. Create a todo for each. Don't manufacture deadlines a brainstorm didn't set.
