This is a team standup. Be terse; standups are scanned, not read.

**Standup notes** — group by person when the transcript makes speakers clear. For each:
what they did, what they're doing next. One or two lines each, no filler.

**Blockers** — anything blocking someone, with who owns unblocking it. Create a todo for each
blocker that needs follow-up. If there were no blockers, say "No blockers raised."

Skip status theater — only capture what actually moves work forward.
